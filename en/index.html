aboutsuccess

About

I started in application development: Java, Python and C++ applications, web interfaces and PostgreSQL migration scripts. Then I moved to product data: analyzing logs, writing loaders and parsers in Go and Python, building aggregates in ClickHouse. Today I develop and evolve ETL/ELT processes, Airflow DAGs, a DWH on Hadoop, Hive and Impala, and data marts and aggregates in ClickHouse and PostgreSQL.

I own the full data lifecycle: collection from internal and external sources, parsing and normalization, enrichment, storage, aggregation, reporting, monitoring, alerting and automation. I design fault-tolerant pipelines, tune query and orchestration performance, and keep track of data quality and SLAs.

I have worked with product, marketing and log data and with integrations with Google, Facebook, AppAnnie, myTracker and other external APIs. I deliver data to the business: dashboards, reports, self-service BI, ad-hoc analysis. In the team I do code review, mentoring and architecture decisions, and work with analytics, product and infrastructure teams.

Tasks

  • extract

    Data collection

    Collecting data from product logs and external sources: Google, Facebook, AppAnnie, myTracker, internal APIs, databases, files, S3. Setting up CDC, exports, parsers and loaders. Working with Kafka and message queues.

  • transform

    Processing and enrichment

    ETL/ELT processes in Python and SQL. Text log parsers, cleansing, normalization, enrichment, deduplication. Data marts, aggregates, SCD2, DWH layers. PySpark, dbt, Spark SQL.

  • load

    Loading and storage

    Loading data into ClickHouse, aggregates in ClickHouse and MySQL. DWH on Hadoop, Hive and Impala. PostgreSQL, S3, HDFS, Parquet. Tuning partitioning, compression and storage schemas.

  • schedule

    Orchestration

    Airflow DAGs: dynamic DAGs, custom operators, sensors, pools, SLAs, retries, backfill. Airflow configuration and performance tuning. CeleryExecutor and KubernetesExecutor, queue and worker monitoring.

  • quality

    Data quality

    Completeness, uniqueness, timeliness and consistency checks. Reconciliation between sources and data marts. Anomaly detection, Great Expectations, dbt tests, custom checks.

  • report

    Reporting and BI

    Report automation, business intelligence dashboards, data analysis for marketing. Superset, Metabase, Grafana, ad-hoc analysis. Self-service BI and a data mart layer for analysts.

  • monitor
